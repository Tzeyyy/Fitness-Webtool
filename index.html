Bangladeshi Diet Plan

Targeted high-protein intake customized for high-metabolism lean bulk

Breakfast (8:00 AM)

~25g Protein
  • 3 Boiled Eggs (2 whole + 1 white)
  • 2 Roti with Peanut Butter OR 1 bowl Milk Oats
  • 1 Banana (Shobri/Sagor)

Lunch (1:30 PM)

~35g Protein
  • 1.5 - 2 cups cooked Rice
  • 150g Chicken Breast OR 150g Fish (Rui/Katla/Tilapia)
  • 1 bowl Thick Lentils (Ghaora Masoor Dal)
  • Green Salad (Cucumber, Tomato)

Post-Workout Snack (5:30 PM)

~20g Protein
  • 1 bowl Boiled Chickpeas (Chola) OR Roasted Peanuts (Badam)
  • 1 cup Sweet Plain Yogurt (Misti Doi) OR Tok Doi with half banana

Dinner (9:00 PM)

~30g Protein
  • 2 Roti OR 1.5 cups Rice
  • 100g Chicken, Fish, or Egg Curry
  • Mixed Vegetables (Shobji)
